# 60HB — v0.14 Stackless Performance Tradeoffs

> **Status:** Accepted 2026-08-27 by explicit operator decision after the first
> clean v0.14 candidate was blocked before publication. This is a one-release
> exception to [60H](60h-stackless-production-acceptance-and-rollout.md), not a
> change to Design 46's normal thresholds or a baseline ratchet.

## Decision

Birnam v0.14 accepts the measured throughput cost of making the completed
stackless runtime, portable CodeObjects, image v8, exact-current admission, and
bounded lifecycle machinery the production system. Stack safety, resumability,
portable executable meaning, and reclaimable live evolution are the benefit;
this release does not claim that the first complete implementation preserves
v0.11-v0.13 throughput.

The accepted evidence is the clean `637419b` candidate on `Mac15,12`, with
Rust-floor dylib SHA-256
`9232fd4c3eca912afd81bbcd97316f0145b2ec0551086b724450f96cc3b8be12`.
It was compared with Design 61A's exact Rust baseline
`bench/results/v0.11.0-rust-2fe44d2-239d348c297583789c4a4da8d5713ebcfd1b9ba1dbf12c04ecb44e56282ac0ea.json`.
That file is the newest published production-floor snapshot because v0.12 and
v0.13 did not complete their documented post-release snapshot step.

## Observed boundary

The ordinary comparator reported 41 regressions, zero missing cases, one
ill-conditioned case, and one new case. Representative medians were:

| Case | Prior | v0.14 candidate | Delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `sends.unary` | 1,436 ns/op | 1,718 ns/op | +19.6% |
| `loops.to-do` | 209 ns/op | 49,574 ns/op | +23,568% |
| `string.scan-derived` | 43.0 ms/op | 3,084 ms/op | +7,068% |
| `query.single-clause` | 1.54 ms/op | 701.7 ms/op | +45,538% |
| `boot.stdlib-dylib` | 19.1 ms | 135.4 ms | +609% |
| `docgen.full` | 1.83 s | 54.36 s | +2,864% |

The new direct-send anchor `sends.compiled-unary` measured 551 ns/op. The
candidate still passed the same-run 60E/60F transition ratios, structural
fast-path checks, million-transition constant-depth tests, 100,000-iteration
callback-control proof, immutable archive lifecycle matrix, and bounded churn.

`map.at-miss` had positive raw timings in all three processes but a negative
median after empty-loop subtraction. For v0.14 only, its net result is
quarantined as ill-conditioned while its presence, operation count, and raw
measurements remain mandatory. It is not evidence of an improvement.

The clean reruns exposed subtraction artifacts in micro cases whose accepted
net work is at most 25% of their raw process time. The frozen subset is
`blocks.value`, `cells.read`, `cells.write`, `error.propagate-5-deep`,
`ffi.peek-poke`, `ffi.wrapped-call`, `map.at-hit`, `sends.compiled-unary`,
`sends.dnu-mint`, `sends.unary`, and `vector.at-random`. Representative raw
medians remained stable even when net medians moved: `ffi.wrapped-call` 8,380
to 8,381 ns and `sends.compiled-unary` 8,391 to 8,307 ns. Because the runtime
dylib was byte-identical, the v0.14 rule bounds this subset's raw medians at the
same 10% allowance instead of amplifying empty-loop subtraction noise. Unlike
`map.at-miss`, every case remains bounded.

`image.save` was bimodal across three runs: 515.5, 442.1, and 438.0 ms in the
accepted candidate versus 507.7, 445.7, and 520.6 ms in the final rerun. Its
worst run was stable even though its median changed. The v0.14 rule therefore
requires all three positive measurements and bounds their maximum at 5% above
the accepted candidate's 515.5 ms maximum.

`boot.stdlib-dylib` likewise retained stable uncontended work across five
repetitions: its minimum moved from 134.8 to 135.5 ms while its final median
jittered to 142.7 ms. Its five-sample minimum is bounded at 5% above the
accepted candidate; the complete macro result remains mandatory.

## Executable acceptance rule

`scripts/v014-performance-acceptance.py` freezes every clean-candidate median,
the exact runtime dylib, machine, prior baseline digest, and complete case set.
A final candidate may exceed the accepted medians only by Design 46's existing
noise allowance: 10% for micro cases and 5% for every other layer. Missing,
renamed, newly unusable, dirty, quick, wrong-machine, or different-runtime
evidence fails closed. The subtraction-sensitive micro subset uses raw medians,
`image.save` uses its maximum individual run, and `boot.stdlib-dylib` uses its
minimum of five repetitions. The sole unbounded net value is `map.at-miss`,
whose complete positive raw measurements are still checked.

After v0.14 is published, its final release receipt and tracked snapshot become
the prior-production baseline. v0.15 and later return to ordinary Design 46 and
60H comparison; they do not inherit this exception. Performance recovery is a
new optimization arc and must preserve the stackless semantic and lifecycle
gates rather than restoring the retired execution model.
