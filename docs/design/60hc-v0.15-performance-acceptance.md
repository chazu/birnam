# 60HC — v0.15 performance acceptance

> **Status:** Accepted 2026-09-09 by explicit operator decision, after the
> first clean v0.15 candidate was blocked at the `performance_compare` gate.
> Like [60HB](60hb-v0.14-performance-tradeoffs.md) this is a one-release
> exception to [60H](60h-stackless-production-acceptance-and-rollout.md); it
> changes neither Design 46's thresholds nor the tracked baseline.

## Decision

Birnam v0.15 is judged by the ordinary Design 46 comparator against the
tracked v0.14.0 snapshot, with exactly one case, `vector.includes-miss`,
bounded by its raw per-operation median rather than its empty-loop-subtracted
net. Every other case keeps its ordinary net threshold; any other flag, any
missing case, a dirty, quick, wrong-machine, or wrong-version candidate, or a
baseline whose bytes differ from the tracked snapshot fails closed.

## Observed boundary

The clean candidate `5f4a4da4` (commit `65194088` plus the release bump) passed
archive integrity, install layout, exact-current rejection, image resave
plateau, stackless image, portable resumable, portable code binding, portable
image v8, stackless control, bounded churn, the host-gated release benchmark,
and the baseline contract. The ordinary comparator then reported one
regression, zero missing cases, and three ill-conditioned cases, with 44 cases
ok or improved:

| `vector.includes-miss` | v0.14.0 baseline | v0.15 candidate |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| net ns/op (three-process median) | 155,769 | 173,546 (+11.4%) |
| raw ns/op (three-process median) | 222,291 | 208,295 (−6.3%) |
| empty-loop baseline subtracted | ≈ 67,800 | ≈ 34,700 |

The case's raw time fell. Its net rose only because v0.15 halved the cost of
the empty `to:do:` loop the harness subtracts (`loops.to-do` 48.8 → 31.0 µs),
so less is subtracted from a case whose net is about 70% of its raw. A direct
interleaved probe of twelve process pairs on the same host measured raw 207 µs
(v0.14.0) against 187 µs (candidate), empty loop 47 against 30 µs, and net
159.7 against 156.8 µs. This is the subtraction artifact 60HB documented for
its raw-bounded subset, now reaching a case that was outside that subset.

The three ill-conditioned cases (`map.at-hit`, `ffi.wrapped-call`,
`sends.compiled-unary`) are the comparator's existing non-fatal treatment of
near-zero nets and are not accepted as improvements.

Evidence of the blocked attempt is retained in
`dist/v0.15.0-stackless-acceptance-failed-5f4a4da4/` (its benchmark, gate
ledger, and comparator log). An earlier attempt, `f88750d8`, failed the
baseline contract because the working tree was edited during the run; it is
retained as `dist/v0.15.0-stackless-acceptance-failed-f88750d8/` and carries no
performance finding.

## Executable acceptance rule

`scripts/v015-performance-acceptance.py` pins the tracked v0.14.0 snapshot by
SHA-256, commit, and machine; requires a clean, full, same-machine v0.15.0
candidate; runs `scripts/bench-compare.sh` unchanged and prints its output; and
then accepts only if the comparator's regression list is empty or consists of
`vector.includes-miss` alone with the candidate's raw median at most 10% above
the baseline's raw median (Design 46's micro allowance). Missing cases and any
other regression reject. `scripts/v015-performance-acceptance-test.py` covers
each branch and runs in the cutover verify gate.
`scripts/stackless-release-acceptance.sh` selects the rule by candidate version,
as it does for v0.14.

## Scope and follow-up

After v0.15 is published, its tracked snapshot becomes the prior-production
baseline and v0.16 returns to the ordinary comparison. The general question
this exposes, that a faster subtracted baseline can flag a case whose raw time
improved, is a Design 46 comparator-policy proposal (raw-aware classification
of net regressions), tracked in Beads and not decided here.
