# 60HC — v0.15 performance acceptance

> **Status:** Accepted 2026-09-09 by explicit operator decision after the first
> clean v0.15 candidate was blocked at the `performance_compare` gate, and
> **widened the same day** by a second operator decision after a second clean
> candidate was blocked by a disjoint set of micro flags. Like
> [60HB](60hb-v0.14-performance-tradeoffs.md) this is a one-release exception
> to [60H](../../design/60h-stackless-production-acceptance-and-rollout.md); it changes
> neither Design 46's thresholds nor the tracked baseline.

## Decision

Birnam v0.15 is judged by the ordinary Design 46 comparator against the
tracked v0.14.0 snapshot, with every **micro-layer** flag re-judged by the
case's raw per-operation median rather than its empty-loop-subtracted net. A
micro flag is accepted when either

1. the candidate's raw median is at most 10% above the baseline's raw median
   (Design 46's micro allowance applied to raw time), or
2. the raw median moved by no more than the larger of the two documents'
   run-to-run raw spreads (max − min over the release run's three micro
   processes), so the flag is not distinguishable from that case's own
   process-to-process noise.

Every case in every other layer keeps its ordinary net threshold; any such
flag, any missing case, any incomplete raw measurement, a case the candidate
carries in more than one layer, a dirty, quick, wrong-machine, or
wrong-version candidate, or a baseline whose bytes differ from the tracked
snapshot fails closed.

## Observed boundary

### First blocked candidate (`5f4a4da4`)

The clean candidate `5f4a4da4` (commit `65194088` plus the release bump) passed
archive integrity, install layout, exact-current rejection, image resave
plateau, stackless image, portable resumable, portable code binding, portable
image v8, stackless control, bounded churn, the host-gated release benchmark,
and the baseline contract. The ordinary comparator then reported one
regression, zero missing cases, and three ill-conditioned cases, with 44 cases
ok or improved:

| `vector.includes-miss` | v0.14.0 baseline | v0.15 candidate |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| net ns/op (three-process median) | 155,769 | 173,546 (+11.4%) |
| raw ns/op (three-process median) | 222,291 | 208,295 (−6.3%) |
| empty-loop baseline subtracted | ≈ 67,800 | ≈ 34,700 |

The case's raw time fell. Its net rose only because v0.15 halved the cost of
the empty `to:do:` loop the harness subtracts (`loops.to-do` 48.8 → 31.0 µs),
so less is subtracted from a case whose net is about 70% of its raw. A direct
interleaved probe of twelve process pairs on the same host measured raw 207 µs
(v0.14.0) against 187 µs (candidate), empty loop 47 against 30 µs, and net
159.7 against 156.8 µs. This is the subtraction artifact 60HB documented for
its raw-bounded subset, now reaching a case that was outside that subset.

The three ill-conditioned cases (`map.at-hit`, `ffi.wrapped-call`,
`sends.compiled-unary`) are the comparator's existing non-fatal treatment of
near-zero nets and are not accepted as improvements.

### Second blocked candidate (`de346939`)

The first rule accepted only `vector.includes-miss`. The next clean candidate
`de346939` (commit `228b85fa` plus the release bump) passed the same eleven
gates and the baseline contract, and `vector.includes-miss` was not flagged.
Four different micro cases were, none of them regressions in raw time:

| case | net median (v0.14.0 → v0.15) | raw median (v0.14.0 → v0.15) | candidate raw spread |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `string.split-native` | 2,789,578 → 3,070,921 (+10.0%) | 2,839,234 → 3,108,562 (+9.5%) | 381,156 |
| `map.at-hit` | 168 → 301 (+78.9%) | 7,831 → 5,601 (−28.5%) | 233 |
| `map.at-miss` | 254 → 352 (+38.4%) | 8,082 → 5,447 (−32.6%) | 593 |
| `vector.reversed` | 1,937,546 → 2,182,625 (+12.6%) | 2,002,250 → 2,230,406 (+11.4%) | 632,578 |

`map.at-hit` and `map.at-miss` are the same subtraction artifact as before on
cases whose net is a few percent of their raw (nets of 84–799 ns against raws
of 5–9 µs across the six processes). `string.split-native` sits at the raw
allowance. `vector.reversed`'s three candidate processes measured 1.86, 2.23
and 2.49 µs/op, a spread almost three times the median delta; the flag is
inside the case's own noise. Two clean candidates flagged disjoint sets of
micro cases with no raw regression in either, which is why the operator
widened the rule to the micro layer rather than adding a second named case.

Evidence of both blocked attempts is retained in
`dist/v0.15.0-stackless-acceptance-failed-5f4a4da4/` and
`dist/v0.15.0-stackless-acceptance-failed-de346939/` (benchmark, gate ledger,
and comparator log each). An earlier attempt, `f88750d8`, failed the baseline
contract because the working tree was edited during the run; it is retained as
`dist/v0.15.0-stackless-acceptance-failed-f88750d8/` and carries no
performance finding.

## Executable acceptance rule

`scripts/v015-performance-acceptance.py` pins the tracked v0.14.0 snapshot by
SHA-256, commit, and machine; requires a clean, full, same-machine v0.15.0
candidate; runs `scripts/bench-compare.sh` unchanged and prints its output; and
then accepts only if every regression the comparator lists is carried by the
candidate in the micro layer alone and each passes the raw-allowance or
run-to-run-spread test above. Missing cases and any other regression reject.
`scripts/v015-performance-acceptance-test.py` covers each branch, including
the retained `de346939` figures, and runs in the cutover verify gate.
`scripts/stackless-release-acceptance.sh` selects the rule by candidate
version, as it does for v0.14.

The widened rule was validated offline against both retained benchmarks
through the real comparator (both accept, with the branch taken printed per
case) rather than by a further release run; the v0.15.0 release run itself
is deferred until the host is free.

## Scope and follow-up

After v0.15 is published, its tracked snapshot becomes the prior-production
baseline and v0.16 returns to the ordinary comparison. The general question
this exposes, that a faster subtracted baseline can flag a case whose raw time
improved, and that single-run micro flags need the run-to-run spread the
release run already measures, is a Design 46 comparator-policy proposal
(raw-aware classification of net regressions), tracked in Beads as
`birnam-dg5` and not decided here.
